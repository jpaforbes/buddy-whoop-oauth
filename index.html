Privacy Policy

16-bit Buddy · WHOOP integration · Effective August 12, 2026

This privacy policy describes how the personal project 16-bit Buddy (“the App”) uses WHOOP account data. The App is a single-user desk display that shows the operator’s own recovery and strain scores. It is not a commercial multi-user product.

1. Who this covers

The App is operated by the individual who registered the WHOOP developer application (contact via the email listed on that developer app). Only that operator’s WHOOP account is intended to be connected.

2. Data we access

With your consent through WHOOP OAuth, the App may request these scopes:

  • read:recovery — recovery score and related recovery metrics
  • read:cycles — physiological cycle data including day strain
  • offline — refresh tokens so the desk can keep working without re-login

The App does not request profile, workout, sleep-detail, or body-measurement scopes.

3. How data is used

  • Recovery percentage and day strain are shown on a local physical display.
  • OAuth access and refresh tokens are stored on the operator’s local machine and/or device non-volatile storage so the App can refresh data periodically.
  • Data is not sold, rented, or used for advertising.
  • Data is not shared with third parties except WHOOP (as the source API) and the network path required to call WHOOP’s API.

4. Storage and retention

  • Tokens and the latest recovery/strain values may be retained on local devices until deleted by the operator or until WHOOP access is revoked.
  • The App does not operate a cloud user database for WHOOP data.

5. Your choices

  • You may deny consent during the WHOOP authorization screen.
  • You may revoke the App’s access at any time in your WHOOP account / connected apps settings.
  • After revocation, the App will no longer be able to fetch WHOOP data with those tokens.

6. Children

The App is not directed to children under 13 and is not intended for their use.

7. Changes

If this policy changes in a material way, the effective date above will be updated and the revised policy will be posted at this URL.

8. Contact

Questions about this policy: use the contact email registered on the WHOOP developer application for 16-bit Buddy.
